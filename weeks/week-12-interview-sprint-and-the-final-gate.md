# Week 12: Interview sprint and the final gate

Days 85 to 88, then the final gate on days 89 to 90. AI off for assessment.

Goal: by day 90 you have designed systems on a whiteboard, told six true stories from your own debug logs, survived three timed break-fix drills with no AI, and defended the capstone against hard questions. Applications have been going out since day 60; this week converts them.

[Back to the week list](../README.md#the-weeks)

## Learn, in this order

1. System design for DevOps, the method: requirements, constraints, components, failure modes, trade-offs
2. The four practice prompts: CI/CD for 20 teams; a highly available web app on AWS; migrating VMs to Kubernetes; monitoring for 50 services
3. The behavioural layer: STAR stories mined from your debug logs and the game day; production issues; disagreements with developers; being new; level and salary conversations
4. Hands-on screen formats: a broken pod, a broken pipeline, broken Terraform; take-home patterns; pairing etiquette; short bash and Python tasks
5. Portfolio: GitHub profile and READMEs with diagrams; LinkedIn from the project log; the CV
6. Application strategy for Nigeria, remote and UK or EU roles; what to say about being new
7. The certification path after hire (AWS Solutions Architect Associate, CKA, Terraform Associate) and why not before

## Tasks

### Day 1 (Mon)
- [ ] Two system designs on a whiteboard, recorded and reviewed against the method

### Day 2 (Tue)
- [ ] Write six STAR stories from your logs, each with the number that changed; rehearse them aloud

### Day 3 (Wed)
- [ ] Three timed break-fix drills, 30 minutes each, no AI, narrating as you go

### Day 4 (Thu)
- [ ] GitHub profile and READMEs with diagrams; LinkedIn and CV reviewed
- [ ] Ten more applications; three mock interviews booked

### The final gate (days 89 to 90, AI and internet off)
- [ ] 90-question oral exam across all twelve weeks; 70 clean answers to pass
- [ ] 45-minute system design on a whiteboard, questioned by a second instructor
- [ ] 30-minute live break-fix on a platform you have never seen
- [ ] Capstone defence: every decision justified; "the AI wrote it" ends the defence

A pass earns the certificate of completion and a referral where a hiring partner exists. A fail earns a targeted two-week extension on the weakest areas and one retake.
